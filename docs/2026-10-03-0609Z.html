Alpenglow Consensus Upgrade on Track for October Mainnet — 150ms Finality, Smaller Validator Threshold

2–3 Oct 2026

Solana’s Alpenglow upgrade — the network’s largest-ever protocol change — is live on devnet and testnet, with the Solana Foundation targeting Agave 4.3 and October 2026 for mainnet activation (no fixed date or block height set).
