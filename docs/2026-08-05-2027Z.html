BlackRock Launches BRSRV Tokenized Stablecoin Reserve Fund on Solana

3 Aug 2026

BlackRock publicly announced on 3 August (SEC filing 31 July) the launch of the BlackRock Daily Reinvestment Stablecoin Reserve Vehicle (BRSRV) alongside tokenized shares of its existing BlackRock Select Treasury-Based Liquidity Fund (BSTBL) — both recorded on Solana, Ethereum, and Tempo. DeFi Development Corp. (DFDV) Announces Support for SIMD-0550 and SIMD-0553 Tokenomics Proposals

4 Aug 2026

NASDAQ-listed DeFi Development Corp. (DFDV) announced on 4 August it will back both SIMD-0550 and SIMD-0553 in the current signaling phase and plans to vote in favour if the proposals advance to a formal on-chain vote. SIMD-0550 would double the annual disinflation rate (15% to 30%), removing ~18.9M SOL in future emissions; SIMD-0553 would replace static fees with a resource-based burn model, increasing daily SOL burns from ~648 SOL to an estimated 7,500–9,000 SOL. CEO Joseph Onorati called the proposals “meaningful steps toward a stronger and more sustainable economic model.” The combined SGP-0003 still needs ~40M more SOL in validator stake by 18 August to reach the 15% signaling threshold. Not investment advice.
