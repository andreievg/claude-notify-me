Solana Completes Final Block Time Reduction to 200ms — SIMD-0525 Fully Activated

9 Oct 2026

Solana activated the final step of SIMD-0525 at epoch 1053 (~15:00 UTC on October 9, 2026), cutting the target slot time from 250ms to 200ms and completing a staged reduction from 400ms that began in August (350ms on Aug 21 → 300ms on Aug 28 → 250ms on Sept 18 → 200ms on Oct 9). The change means the network now targets five block-production opportunities per second (up from 2.5), which trading apps, wallets, and exchanges may benefit from through more frequent state updates and reduced exposure to stale prices. Trade-offs include a lower per-block compute limit (30M vs 37.5M CUs), shorter validator ordering windows (800ms vs 1.6s), and higher validator voting costs. CoinDesk reports SOL showing ~$109.15 on its live ticker; the article itself does not attribute any price move to the upgrade. This is a significant and long-awaited network milestone, distinct from the Alpenglow upgrade (which targets 150ms finality and remains on testnet). Not investment advice.
