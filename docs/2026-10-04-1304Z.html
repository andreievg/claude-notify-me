Alpenglow Upgrade Delayed to October — Mainnet “Alpenswitch” Timing Still Uncertain

3–4 Oct 2026

Solana’s Alpenglow consensus upgrade — replacing Proof of History and Tower BFT with Votor and Rotor to cut finality from 12.8 seconds to ~150ms — was delayed from September and is now targeting October 2026 with the Agave 4.3 release. The “Alpenswitch” to mainnet cannot activate until enough validators register BLS public keys and the Validator Admission Ticket (VAT) is active; no exact date within October has been set. The upgrade also eliminates on-chain vote transactions (currently 75% of block space), significantly expanding real-transaction capacity. Not investment advice.
