Western Union Launches USDPT Stablecard on Solana, Enabling Stablecoin Spending at 175M Merchant Locations

4 Aug 2026

Western Union and payments fintech Rain launched the USDPT Stablecard on 4 August — a Visa card backed by USDPT (US Dollar Payment Token), a GENIUS Act-compliant stablecoin issued by federally chartered Anchorage Digital Bank and running on Solana. The card pairs a USDPT-denominated digital wallet with a Visa secured credit card, allowing users to receive Western Union remittances directly in USDPT and spend at any of Visa’s 175 million merchant locations, with Apple Pay and Google Pay compatibility. The product is launching in 37 markets, expanding to 60+ by end of year. The launch brings Western Union’s $107 billion global payment network into the Solana ecosystem as a regulated, real-world use case. Not investment advice.

Agave 4.2 Mainnet Activation Confirmed for 17 August: 200ms Slots, 90% Rent Cut, 4,096-Byte Transactions

5 Aug 2026

Solana client developer Anza confirmed that the Agave 4.2 upgrade will activate on mainnet on 17 August 2026, after XDP (eXpress Data Path) networking support cleared its supermajority threshold. The upgrade halves slot times from 400ms to 200ms — doubling the network’s block confirmation speed — cuts rent costs by 90%, and raises maximum transaction size from 1,232 to 4,096 bytes (a 3.3x increase). Agave 4.2 also ships the complete code for the Alpenglow consensus protocol (Solana’s largest-ever protocol redesign), though Alpenglow itself won’t activate until Agave 4.3. Turbine retransmit latency was improved from ~600ms to ~0.8ms via XDP. Not investment advice.
