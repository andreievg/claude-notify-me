Japan’s SBI Holdings and SMFG Launch SBI Solana Global Venture for Stablecoin Payments and Tokenisation

25 Nov 2026

SBI Holdings, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group (SMFG), and the Solana Foundation formally launched SBI Solana Global on 25 November 2026, converting the existing SBI R3 Japan entity into the new joint venture. The venture’s stated focus areas are stablecoin payment rails (domestic and cross-border, including potential AI agent micropayments), tokenisation of real assets and financial instruments, and on-chain asset management — starting in Japan and expanding across Asia. Anza Unveils 2027 Solana Roadmap: Double Compute Capacity, Cut Token-Account Costs 80%

19–26 Nov 2026

Anza, the core Solana engineering team, outlined its 2027 technical roadmap at Breakpoint 2026 in London (15–17 November) and in reporting through 26 November. The plan targets roughly doubling compute capacity from ~250 M to ~500 M compute units per second by end-2027 and an 80% reduction in compute needed for token-account transactions, which would lower fees and free bandwidth. A separate storage-cost track (SIMD-0437) aims for a ~90% cumulative reduction in storage expenses through phased rent cuts. Additional scope includes expanded transaction account limits, a new block-distribution component called Constellation, and protocol simplification. These are engineering targets, not completed work; no precise fee-impact schedule has been published. Not investment advice.
