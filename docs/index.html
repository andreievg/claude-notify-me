Transaction V1 Mainnet Activation in 2 Days (Sep 9): Final Readiness Confirmed

5–6 Sep 2026

Solana’s Transaction V1 upgrade (SIMD-0296 + SIMD-0385) is confirmed to activate on mainnet on 9 Sep — two days from this run.
