KSNET × Solana Foundation MOU: Solana Pay and x402 AI Micropayments Piloted Across 330,000+ South Korean Merchants

30 Jul 2026

South Korean payment processor KSNET and the Solana Foundation signed a memorandum of understanding in Seoul on 30 July to pilot Solana Pay across KSNET’s 330,000+ merchant network, which processes ~130 million transactions and ~$4 billion in monthly volume. The MOU covers two proof-of-concept tracks: integrating Solana Pay into online and offline payment flows, and trialing the x402 protocol — which uses the HTTP “Payment Required” status code to enable AI agents to autonomously execute micropayments for API access and digital content. The deal builds on a previous Solana Foundation $100M fund for South Korean web3 startups and leverages South Korea’s recently liberalised Virtual Asset User Protection Act. Analysts describe it as one of the largest real-world crypto payment integrations in Asia. Not investment advice.
