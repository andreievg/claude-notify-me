Solana’s First Governance Vote Opens Today — Solana Company Opposes SGP-0003 on Timing

22 Aug 2026

On-chain voting for Solana’s first three governance proposals opened 22 August. The proposals include SGP-0001 (the Solana Constitution), SGP-0002, and SGP-0003 (resource and inclusion fee structure, SIMD-0553 + SIMD-0550). Solana Company — a major institutional validator — announced it will vote FOR SGP-0001 but AGAINST SGP-0003, arguing that introducing variable resource fees prematurely shifts risk to users and operators before the ecosystem has the budgeting tools to absorb it; the company does not oppose the underlying goal and would reconsider with a deterministic fee floor. If SGP-0003 passes, daily SOL burns rise from ~650 to ~9,000 tokens (~14×), the annual disinflation rate doubles to 30%, and the terminal supply date moves from 2032 to 2029. A two-thirds supermajority of active stake is required to pass each proposal. Not investment advice.
