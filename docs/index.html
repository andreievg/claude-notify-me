Alpenglow Mainnet Activation Tomorrow (21 Oct): Epoch 1064, ~15:00 UTC

18–19 Oct 2026

Solana’s Alpenglow consensus upgrade — replacing TowerBFT (in use since 2020) with Votor for voting and Rotor for block propagation — is confirmed to activate on mainnet at epoch 1064 on 21 October at approximately 14:50–15:00 UTC. The feature gate flips in a single slot; TowerBFT remains in control for ~5,000 slots (~18 minutes) as a safety net before Votor takes over. Target finality drops from ~12.8 seconds to 100–150 milliseconds (a target, not yet demonstrated on mainnet at scale). Validators must update to Agave 4.1 before activation; wallet users and developers need take no action. The upgrade (SIMD-0326) passed validator governance with 98.27% approval. Not investment advice.
