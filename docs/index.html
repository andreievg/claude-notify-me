Samsung Wallet Taps Solana for USDC Transfers on 82M U.S. Galaxy Devices

7 Oct 2026

Samsung announced on October 7, 2026 that Samsung Wallet and Samsung Pay will add USDC stablecoin transfers on Solana (and Sui) for 82 million compatible U.S. Galaxy devices, rolling out in the last week of October 2026. Users can send USDC to external crypto wallets at no Samsung fee, or convert and deliver funds to bank accounts in more than 60 countries in local currency (bank transfer fees apply). Custody is handled by Bastion and Coinbase Prime; users do not need to manage private keys or a separate crypto app. Eligibility checks and ID verification are required. The Solana Foundation issued its own announcement alongside Samsung’s, marking the integration as a major mainstream on-ramp for Solana-based stablecoins. Not investment advice.

10 Oct 2026

If adopted, the change would double the computational work validators process per unit time and could require hardware upgrades for some operators. Not investment advice.
