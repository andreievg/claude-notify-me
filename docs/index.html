Alpenglow Goes Live on Solana Mainnet, Cutting Finality to ~150ms

21 Oct 2026

Solana’s Alpenglow upgrade — the network’s largest consensus overhaul since 2020 — activated on mainnet-beta on 21 October 2026 at approximately 14:50 UTC (epoch 1064 boundary), as scheduled by Anza. The Votor protocol replaces TowerBFT, moving consensus votes off-chain and targeting finality of ~150 milliseconds versus ~12.8 seconds previously. The upgrade also replaces per-transaction vote fees with a flat 0.8 SOL per epoch validator fee. Only the first phase (Votor) launched on 21 October; the second phase (Rotor) does not yet have a confirmed date. The launch followed six testnet restarts and a rolled-back migration during testing. No post-launch network incidents were reported in real-time coverage. Not investment advice.
