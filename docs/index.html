Alpenglow Consensus Upgrade Activates 21 Oct at ~15:00 UTC (Epoch 1064) — 4 Days Away

14–17 Oct 2026

Solana’s Alpenglow upgrade is confirmed to activate on mainnet at approximately 15:00 UTC on 21 October 2026 (epoch 1064), replacing TowerBFT (in use since 2020) with the Votor and Rotor protocols. Key changes: transaction finality drops from ~12.8 seconds to ~150 milliseconds; fault-tolerance threshold rises from 33% to 40%; vote transactions move off-chain, freeing block space. Validators must update to Agave 4.1 before activation; the transition takes one slot, with TowerBFT remaining active for 18 minutes as a safety buffer. The proposal (SIMD-0326) passed with 98.27% validator approval in September 2025. Regular users and developers need no action — fees, VM, and transaction formats are unchanged. The upgrade has not yet functioned as a near-term SOL price catalyst. Not investment advice.
