Solana Foundation Launches DvP Institutional Settlement Tool With JPMorgan Input

6 Oct 2026

The Solana Foundation launched Solana DvP on approximately October 5–6, 2026 — an open-source atomic delivery-versus-payment program that lets institutions settle tokenised assets and cash simultaneously on Solana, cutting settlement time from T+1/T+2 to seconds. JPMorgan’s markets digital assets head Rhodel D’Souza provided input on institutional requirements and called it “exactly the kind of foundational infrastructure institutional market participants require,” though JPMorgan is not endorsing or operating the tool. The escrow program supports Token-2022 features (transfer restrictions, pause), has passed external security audits, and is MIT-licensed for any counterparty pair to use. The Solana Foundation said it plans to add confidential settlement features. Not investment advice.
