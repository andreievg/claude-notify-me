BlackRock Launches BRSRV Tokenized Money Market Fund on Solana — Designed for Stablecoin Reserve Management

3 Aug 2026

BlackRock launched the BlackRock Daily Reinvestment Stablecoin Reserve Vehicle (BRSRV) on 3 August 2026, recording ownership on Solana, Ethereum, and Tempo via whitelisted wallets managed by transfer agent Securitize. The fund invests entirely in cash, short-term U.S. Treasuries, and overnight repos, and is specifically designed to qualify as an eligible reserve asset for payment stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act; a $3M minimum investment is required. This represents BlackRock’s second major on-chain tokenization move beyond its BUIDL fund and significantly raises Solana’s profile as a permissioned institutional settlement layer. Not investment advice.
