Alpenglow Upgrade Scheduled for 21 Oct at ~14:50 UTC (Epoch 1064) — Five Days Away

14–15 Oct 2026

Anza has confirmed Solana’s Alpenglow consensus upgrade will activate on mainnet at approximately 14:50 UTC on 21 October 2026, tied to epoch 1064. The upgrade replaces TowerBFT (active since 2020) with the Votor and Rotor protocols, targeting finality of approximately 150 milliseconds (down from ~12.8 seconds) and raising fault tolerance from 33% to 40%. Vote transactions will move off-chain, freeing block space, though reported on-chain transaction counts may visibly decline as a result. CoinDesk described it as the most significant consensus overhaul in Solana’s history. Regular SOL holders need take no action. The upgrade is a confirmed positive for Solana fundamentals, though current macro conditions have so far prevented it from acting as a price catalyst. Not investment advice.
