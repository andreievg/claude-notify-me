Alpenglow Mainnet Upgrade Confirmed for 21 October — Five Days Away; Epoch 1064 Activation at ~14:50 UTC

14 Oct 2026

Anza confirmed that Solana’s Alpenglow consensus upgrade will activate on mainnet-beta on 21 October 2026 at approximately 14:50 UTC, tied to epoch 1064. The upgrade replaces TowerBFT (in use since March 2020) with the Votor and Rotor protocols, targeting finality of ~150 milliseconds (down from ~12.8 seconds) and raising fault tolerance from 33% to 40%. Validator voting transactions will move off-chain, freeing block space — though this may cause a visible decline in reported on-chain transaction counts. Validators must run Agave 4.3.0; regular SOL holders need take no action. CoinDesk described the change as the most significant consensus overhaul in Solana’s history. Current macro conditions are preventing the upgrade from acting as a positive price catalyst. Not investment advice.
