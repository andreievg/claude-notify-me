Alpenglow Upgrade 5 Days Away — Confirmed for 21 Oct at ~15:00 UTC (Epoch 1064)

15–16 Oct 2026

Anza and the Solana Foundation have confirmed that the Alpenglow consensus upgrade will activate on mainnet at approximately 15:00 UTC on 21 October 2026 (epoch 1064), replacing TowerBFT (active since 2020) with the Votor and Rotor protocols. The upgrade targets finality of ~150 milliseconds (down from ~12.8 seconds), raises fault tolerance from 33% to 40%, and moves vote transactions off-chain to free block space. TowerBFT will remain active for 18 minutes after the switch to ensure stability. The VM, transaction format, and fees are unchanged; regular SOL holders need take no action. CoinDesk has called it the most significant consensus overhaul in Solana’s history. It has not yet acted as a near-term price catalyst. Not investment advice.
