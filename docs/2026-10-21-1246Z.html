Alpenglow Activates on Solana Mainnet, Cutting Finality Target to ~150ms

21 Oct 2026

Solana’s Alpenglow upgrade went live on mainnet on 21 October 2026, replacing the TowerBFT consensus mechanism with Votor — a system that moves validator votes off-chain and bundles results into certificates, targeting finality of ~150 milliseconds versus the prior ~6–12.8 seconds. The epoch 1064 activation occurred at approximately 13:20–14:50 UTC (sources differ slightly). Validator costs are expected to fall roughly 60%, with a flat 0.8 SOL per-epoch admission fee replacing approximately 2 SOL per day in vote fees. Execution, the SVM, transaction fees, and application logic are unchanged. The upgrade follows six testnet restarts and a rolled-back migration during testing; no post-launch network incidents were reported. SOL price remained stable on the day at ~$105.75, suggesting the upgrade was already priced in. Not investment advice.
