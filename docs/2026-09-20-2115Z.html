SEC Issues Five-Year “Innovation Exemption” for Tokenised Stock Trading — Solana Named Biggest Winner

17–18 Sep 2026

The SEC issued a five-year conditional exemption on 17 September allowing Tokenised Securities Venues (TSVs) to trade tokenised NMS stocks via permissioned automated market makers, without registering as traditional exchanges. Rules require tokenised shares to carry identical shareholder rights to their underlying stock, smart contracts to be publicly auditable on a permissionless ledger, and trading to halt if the underlying stock halts on its primary exchange. Solana was immediately identified as the primary beneficiary: $465M in tokenised equities are already trading on-chain there, the most of any blockchain, and the two-year Solana Foundation effort to build out this infrastructure now has explicit regulatory backing. The exemption arrived just two days after the CLARITY Act failed in the Senate, signalling a shift to agency-level rule-making. Not investment advice.

Solana Cuts Slot Time to 250ms, Boosting Block Production Frequency by 17%

18 Sep 2026

Solana activated the third stage of SIMD-0525 on 18 September, reducing its target slot time from 300ms to 250ms and increasing block production frequency by approximately 17% (from ~3.3 to ~4 slots per second). The change does not raise raw transaction throughput; instead, compute and data limits per slot were scaled down proportionally so the network produces smaller blocks more often, allowing wallets and apps to receive updates faster. Network stability concerns exist — shorter slots tighten validator coordination windows — and the roadmap targets a further reduction to 200ms. Not investment advice.
